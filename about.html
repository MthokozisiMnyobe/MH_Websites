About MH Websites

Local understanding. Practical technology. Long-term ambition.

MH Websites is a South African digital-solutions company helping organisations work smarter and present themselves professionally.

Our story

Created to help businesses use technology with confidence.

MH Websites was established in 2024 in East London, Eastern Cape. What began with website development grew into a wider technology offering: custom business systems, e-commerce, learning platforms, dashboards, IT support and computer supplies.

We believe technology should remove friction—not add another complicated tool. That means listening carefully, building around real workflows and staying available when the solution goes live.
